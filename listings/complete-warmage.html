Complete Warmage

Warmages wield cantrips as swiftly and efficiently as a fighter wields weapons. It’s no accident they’ve claimed the names of chess pieces for themselves. Chess is a game of strategy, where greater pieces trump lesser ones, and everything is done in service to victory. Logic alone dictates the winner, just as it does with magic.

The Complete Warmage offers one of the most customizable, easy to pick up spellcasters out there. Pick your cantrips, and enhance them with Warmage Tricks. Pick an Arcane Initiation, a Fighting Style, and pick a House—either one themed off chess pieces, like the House of Kings, or one based on gambling, like the House of Cards, House of Roulette, or House of Darts.

Note: The Warmage class is available using the free Core Classes module, which also contains a number of automations to help make playing a Warmage easier in Foundry.
