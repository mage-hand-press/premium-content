Complete Gunslinger

The pen might be mightier than the sword, but it don’t hold a candle to my peace-keeper.

The Complete Gunslinger contains the definitive version of perhaps Mage Hand Press’s most fun and magnetic class to date, brought wholesale with an arsenal of weapons to the 2024 version of the rules.

This class comes with 14 subclasses for gunslingers of every stripe, from the Elephant-Gun-wielding Big Game Hunter to the cool-as-ice Space Cowboy.

This master of gunplay comes packaged with an extensive list of firearms, ranging from Muskets and Assault Rifles, to Grenade Launchers and futuristic blasters—everything perfectly balanced with other options in 5th Edition. If you haven’t played with the Mage Hand Press firearm rules at your table, you should pick this book up for these guns alone!

Lastly, the book contains a number of options for other characters: feats and spells, so that other characters can enjoy a bit of gunslinging fun, without jumping into the Gunslinger wholesale.

Note: The Gunslinger class is available using the free Core Classes module, which also contains a number of automations to help make playing a Gunslinger easier in Foundry.
