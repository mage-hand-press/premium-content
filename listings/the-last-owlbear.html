Overview

There’s always another monster—until there isn’t. In a future fantasy world in which owlbears have been hunted to the edge of extinction, you are tasked with capturing, protecting, and transporting the very last owlbear in the world. Perhaps you can save the monster’s future, or perhaps the owlbear is already a living memory.

The Last Owlbear is a compelling adventure module for 5th-level characters in which the fate of one of D&D’s most iconic monsters is in your hands.

Adventure Synopsis

The adventure begins in Chapter 1: The Caravan, in which the party accompanies a northbound caravan toward the remote Mosswood and confronts a variety of roadside encounters along the way.

At the edge of the Mosswood, the caravan holds a bonfire night to rest and prepare for Chapter 2: The Hunt. The characters are enlisted for the multi-stage plan to capture the owlbear.

Chapter 3: Dangerous Cargo sees the characters retracing their steps on a southbound trek, but now transporting the 1,500-pound owlbear and enduring ambushes from the ruthless Bramblethorn Company, a band of adventurers that wants the owlbear dead.

Finally, in Chapter 4: The Experiment, the characters assist the alchemist in a series of wildly unconventional alchemical experiments in an attempt to replicate a suitable mate to the last owlbear and perpetuate the species.
