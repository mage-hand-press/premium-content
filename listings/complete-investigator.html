Complete Investigator

It seems every evil thing was detailed in that book. Then I saw my own face staring back at me on the page.

Supernatural detectives and monster slayers, Investigators are always on the hunt for malevolent outsiders. Whenever evil seeps into the world—be it Fiends, Undead, or strange Aberrations from beyond the stars—Investigators will be the first to locate them and banish their foul corruption from the mortal plane.

The Complete Investigator is the definitive version of our Investigator class, fully updated to the 2024 edition of the rules! Investigators have never been more dangerous, with Weapon Masteries, new and streamlined rules for Exploit Weakness and Finisher, and more uses of Trinkets and Rushed Incantation.

Live out your very own John Constantine, Hellboy, or Supernatural mystery, with one of thirteen subclasses, and enjoy a spellbook full of new spells and rituals to menace vampires and werewolves. The Complete Investigator also comes with an arsenal of new magic items to help you investigate and slay whatever monster the GM throws at you.

Note: The Investigator class is available using the free Core Classes module, which also contains a number of automations to help make playing a Investigator easier in Foundry.
