Embark on Strange Owlchemy, an exhilarating starter adventure for 1st to 3rd level characters, in which you protect a small town from an attack of owl-creatures and investigate a mad scientist’s attempt to recreate the iconic owlbear. Meet engaging characters in the High Moon tavern, survive the horror of Nocturne Burrow, and investigate an abandoned manor to confront the dreaded owlchemist!

Adventure Synopsis

In Chapter One: Night of the Owl-Beasts!, the party gets to know each other and the locals at the High Moon, a busy tavern at the small crossroads town of Gulliver’s Rest. After some under-the-table gambling and other tavern shenanigans, the town is attacked by a small army of half-owl animals that emerge from the nearby fields.

Chapter Two: Horror of Nocturne Burrow! takes the characters through a small dungeon to discover the source of the attacks. In addition to uncovering a strange new breed of owlgoblins, the characters are left with more questions than answers and likely return home with a townsperson who was captured the night before. However, their journey is interrupted by a terrible wereowl that pursues them to the town’s edge!

By Chapter Three: Laboratory of the Owl-Man!, the characters have deduced that the old Sova Manor must be the source of the alchemical owl-monster creations. They explore the manor and must solve a complicated alchemical formula to confront the owlchemist, a scheming noble who has gone mad from his experiments!
